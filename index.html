About Our Clinic

Empowering Healthy Living Through Movement

Welcome to Fit Life Physiotherapy

At Fit Life, we believe in a patient-first approach to rehabilitation. Led by Dr. Miloni Shah, our clinic has earned a reputation in Surat for providing exceptional, customized orthopedic and neurological physical therapy. We combine evidence-based clinical practice with modern treatment techniques to treat the root cause of your pain.

Our Mission

To deliver target-oriented, high-quality physiotherapy care that speeds up recovery and restores long-term mobility.

Our Vision

To be Surat's leading center of physical wellness, inspiring every individual to maintain active, pain-free lifespans.

Our Services

Expert Therapy Specialized for Your Needs

Back Pain Treatment

Relieve lower and upper back stiffness, disc slips, and sciatica with spinal alignments and muscle strengthening.

Neck Pain

Advanced treatments for cervical spondylosis, text-neck, and muscle spasm using custom neck exercises and postures.

Sports Injury

Fast recovery and performance enhancement for athletes with ligament tears, muscle sprains, and joint instability.

Knee Pain

Relief from osteoarthritis, ACL issues, patellar tendonitis, and age-related stiffness without early surgical interventions.

Shoulder Pain

Restore full mobility for frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), rotator cuff tears, and shoulder impingement.

Arthritis Management

Improve joint integrity, reduce local swelling, and increase muscle support for Rheumatoid Arthritis and Osteoarthritis.

Post Surgery Rehabilitation

Step-by-step guidance after Knee/Hip replacement, ligament reconstruction, and spine surgery for complete rehab.

Paralysis Rehabilitation

Neurological recovery plans for stroke, brain injuries, facial palsy, and nerve compression to regain independence.

Dry Needling

Intramuscular stimulation (IMS) targeting deep muscle myofascial trigger points to release tight muscle fibers immediately.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on joint mobilization, manipulation, and soft tissue release techniques for immediate flexibility and pain reduction.

Cupping Therapy

Traditional and modern myofascial decompression cupping to increase local blood flow, relax tissues, and heal sore muscles.

Home Visit Physiotherapy

Get premium physical rehabilitation in the comfort of your home. Perfect for post-operative recovery and elderly patients.

Kinesio Taping

Relieve joint pressure, support weak muscles, and improve local lymphatic drainage using specialized elastic therapeutic tapes.

Fitness & Pilates

Premium core strength development, flexibility, postural correction, and full-body alignment programs guided by physiotherapists.

Post Cardiac Rehab

Supervised cardiorespiratory exercise plans, lifestyle coaching, and safe heart rate conditioning following heart procedures.

Diet & Nutrition

Clinical nutrition consulting, customized diet charts, and metabolic plans to complement physical therapy and weight management.

Pregnancy Rehab

Specialized prenatal and postnatal back care, pelvic floor conditioning, core stability workouts, and postures for mothers.
